Feedback

Bug, feature idea, or just a thought about the site? There are a few ways to get it to me.

Direct email

For anything that doesn't fit Discord, GitHub, or the form below, you can email me directly at {{ contactEmail }}.

Discord

The fastest way to reach me and the rest of the small TypeFast.io community is on Discord. Bug reports, feature requests, language suggestions, and changelogs all happen there.

GitHub

For bug reports and feature requests that benefit from being trackable and searchable, open an issue on the GitHub issue tracker. Issues are the canonical place to follow what's planned and what's been resolved. See the contribute page if you also want to help fix something yourself.

Anonymous form

If you'd rather drop a comment without signing in to anything, there's a no-account Google Form that goes straight to my inbox.

What makes a useful bug report

The most fixable reports include:

  • What you did, step by step.
  • What you expected to happen.
  • What actually happened.
  • Your browser and operating system (e.g. "Firefox 122 on macOS").
  • A screenshot or short screen recording, if it's a visual issue.

Vague reports ("it's broken") are hard to act on. Concrete ones get fixed quickly.
